Red Naomi Rose Nutrient Management and Fertigation Strategies in Glass Greenhouse Cultivation

In glass greenhouse cultivation of Red Naomi roses, nutrient management directly influences harvest quality and market value. Precise control of fertigation, EC, pH, calcium availability, and micronutrient balance across vegetative growth, bud initiation, and flowering stages determines stem strength, bud uniformity, colour intensity, and vase life potential. As harvest approaches, nutrient steering and irrigation timing become critical for achieving export-grade stem length, tight buds, and post-harvest resilience under intensive greenhouse production systems
