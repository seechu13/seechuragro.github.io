Major Dairy Institutions in India: Knowledge, Policy & Capacity-Building Pillars

India’s dairy success is not accidental; it is the outcome of decades of institutional support, research, cooperative development, and professional education. From farmer-owned cooperatives to national research institutes and management schools, these institutions form the backbone of India’s milk economy. This chapter presents a concise, reference-oriented overview of major dairy institutions in India, outlining their roles, contributions, and relevance for modern dairy farming and agribusiness development.
