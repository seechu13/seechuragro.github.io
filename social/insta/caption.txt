Milk Production Systems: Lactation Biology, Yield Optimization, and Efficiency Control in Modern Dairy Herds

Milk production is the final biological expression of genetics, nutrition, health, and management acting in synchrony. Modern dairy systems no longer view milk yield as a simple outcome of feeding and milking, but as a tightly regulated physiological process governed by endocrine control, mammary cell dynamics, metabolic balance, and environmental stability. This chapter presents a PhD-grade, systems-level exploration of lactation biology, milk synthesis mechanisms, production curves, and management strategies required to sustainably optimize yield, efficiency, and milk quality.
