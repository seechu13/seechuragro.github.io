Arugula (rocket) cultivation under NFT hydroponic system: protected farming practices, nutrient management, and yield potential

This article presents a research-grade protocol for cultivating arugula (Eruca sativa) under NFT hydroponic systems. It covers botanical characteristics, climate requirements, NFT system design, nursery management, nutrient and irrigation scheduling, pest and disease control, harvesting practices, and yield expectations. The guide is designed for commercial and semi-commercial growers seeking rapid crop cycles, uniform leaf quality, and efficient resource use under protected cultivation.
