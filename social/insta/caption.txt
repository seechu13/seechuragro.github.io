Green & Dry Fodder Systems for Dairy Cattle: Production, Storage, and Feeding Strategies in Indian Conditions

Green and dry fodders form the biological foundation of dairy nutrition in India, directly influencing milk yield, animal health, rumen stability, and farm economics. This chapter presents a PhD-grade, India-specific analysis of fodder production systems, including seasonal green fodders, dry crop residues, hydroponic green feeds, and fodder storage methods ranging from traditional village practices to advanced mechanized systems. Emphasis is placed on nutritional value, practical feasibility, cost–benefit realism, and sustainable fodder planning for dairy farms of all scales.
