Pak Choi (Bok Choy) cultivation under NFT hydroponic system: botanical traits, nutrient management, and commercial yield potential

This article presents a research-grade cultivation protocol for Pak Choi (Brassica rapa subsp. chinensis) grown under NFT hydroponic systems. It covers botanical characteristics, nursery management, NFT system design, climate requirements, nutrient and irrigation management, pest and disease control, harvesting practices, and yield potential. The guide is tailored for commercial and semi-commercial growers seeking fast crop cycles, uniform leafy produce, and efficient space and water use under protected cultivation
