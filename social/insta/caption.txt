Milk Pasteurization & Processing Systems: Food Safety, Shelf Life, and Scale Economics

Pasteurization is the most critical food safety intervention in the dairy value chain, transforming raw milk into a safe, stable, and market-ready product. Beyond simple heating, milk processing integrates microbiology, thermal engineering, equipment hygiene, and scale economics. This chapter provides a PhD-grade, systems-based analysis of milk pasteurization and processing—covering scientific principles, processing technologies, plant layouts, and operational realities across small, medium, and large dairy enterprises under Indian conditions
