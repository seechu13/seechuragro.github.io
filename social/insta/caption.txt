Lakadong Turmeric under Protected Cultivation – Part 3: Botany, Growth Physiology, and Curcumin Biosynthesis

This article examines the botanical structure, growth physiology, and curcumin biosynthesis mechanisms of turmeric (Curcuma longa L.) with specific relevance to high-curcumin Lakadong germplasm. It explains how plant morphology, growth stages, and metabolic pathways influence rhizome development and curcumin accumulation. The section provides a scientific foundation for understanding why environmental control and precise crop management are critical for maximizing both yield quality and biochemical consistency in protected turmeric cultivation systems.
