Jalapeño cultivation in grow bag system: protected farming practices, fertigation, and economic viability

This article provides a research-grade guide to jalapeño pepper cultivation using grow bags under protected conditions. It covers variety selection, grow bag media composition, planting density, climate and fertigation management, pruning, pest and disease control, yield expectations, and an indicative cost–return analysis. The guide is tailored for Indian polyhouse and shade-net systems focusing on consistent pungency, fruit uniformity, and extended harvest periods.
