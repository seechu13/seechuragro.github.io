Scientific Dairy Housing, Shed Design & Farm Layouts for High-Performance Dairy Farming

Dairy housing is more than a shed — it is an engineered environment designed to maximize milk production, reduce disease, enhance comfort, and increase operational efficiency. This chapter provides a detailed, research-backed guide to shed structures, ventilation dynamics, flooring materials, drainage gradients, cow comfort designs, and complete farm layouts for 20–200+ animal dairy enterprises.
