Gerbera cultivation under polyhouse: varietal traits, climate management, and commercial cut flower production

This article presents a research-grade cultivation protocol for Gerbera (Gerbera jamesonii) grown under polyhouse conditions. It covers botanical characteristics, variety selection, polyhouse design, climate and light management, growing media, fertigation practices, pest and disease management, harvesting standards, and yield potential. The guide is designed for commercial floriculture growers seeking consistent flower quality, extended harvest cycles, and economically viable cut flower production under protected cultivation
