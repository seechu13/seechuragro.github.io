Dairy Breeds of India: Cows & Buffaloes, Their Traits and Regional Suitability

Breed selection is a critical determinant of dairy farm success, influencing productivity, costs, and long-term sustainability. In Indian conditions, aligning breed characteristics with local climate, feed resources, and management capacity is essential. Indigenous cattle offer resilience, disease resistance, and efficiency under low-input systems, while crossbred and exotic animals provide higher yields but require intensive care. Buffaloes contribute significantly with high-fat milk suited for value-added products. Rather than focusing solely on peak production, farmers should prioritize consistency, adaptability, and system compatibility. Ultimately, successful dairying depends on choosing breeds that match geography, resources, and operational capability.
