Lakadong Turmeric under Protected Cultivation – Part 8: Irrigation, Moisture Control, and Water Productivity

This article examines irrigation management and moisture control strategies for Lakadong turmeric under polyhouse cultivation. It explains the sensitivity of turmeric rhizomes to excess moisture, the physiological consequences of water stress, and the role of drip irrigation in stabilising root-zone conditions. The section highlights how precise irrigation scheduling improves water productivity, reduces disease incidence, and supports consistent curcumin accumulation in high-value Lakadong turmeric grown under protected environments.
