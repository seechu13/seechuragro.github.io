About Seechur Agro

Seechur Agro Private Limited is a sustainable agriculture venture headquartered in Chennai, India. We focus on hydroponic cultivation of vegetables and high-curcumin Lakadong Turmeric from Edayarpakkam, Tamil Nadu.

Our Core Focus

  • Hydroponic cultivation under polyhouses and greenhouses.
  • Production and processing of high-curcumin Lakadong turmeric.
  • Technology-driven, sustainable farming practices.
  • Traceable farm-to-market value chain.

Facilities

The company owns 13.5 acres in Edayarpakkam, with 4 acres under polyhouse and 1.25 acres under greenhouse. From the third year, an additional 6 acres will be allocated for Lakadong turmeric cultivation.

Our Philosophy

“Human by chance, Farmer by choice.”
We believe sustainable agriculture combines tradition with innovation.
