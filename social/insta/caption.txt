Spinach cultivation under NFT hydroponic system: botanical characteristics, climate requirements, and commercial production practices

Spinach (Spinacia oleracea L.) is a fast-growing leafy vegetable well suited to NFT hydroponic systems due to its shallow root structure and short crop cycle. This article presents a research-based guide covering botanical traits, climate and nutrient requirements, NFT system design, planting density, irrigation management, pest control, and yield expectations for professional hydroponic spinach production under controlled environments
