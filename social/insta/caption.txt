Lakadong Turmeric under Protected Cultivation – Part 9: Pest, Disease, and Soil Biology Management

This article examines pest and disease dynamics in Lakadong turmeric under polyhouse cultivation, with emphasis on soil-borne pathogens and biological soil health. The section positions integrated disease and soil biology management as a preventive, system-level approach essential for producing residue-compliant, high-curcumin Lakadong turmeric for premium markets.
