Biogas & Circular Energy Systems in Dairy Farming: Manure-to-Energy, Nutrient Recycling, and Farm Sustainability

Biogas systems transform dairy manure from a disposal challenge into a strategic resource for energy generation and soil fertility. In modern dairy farming, biogas is not an add-on technology but a core component of circular production systems that reduce energy costs, stabilize nutrient cycles, and enhance environmental sustainability. This chapter presents a PhD-grade, India-specific analysis of biogas science, plant design, farm-scale sizing, energy utilization pathways, and slurry management, integrating dairying with renewable energy and sustainable agriculture.
