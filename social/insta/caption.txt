Polyhouse floriculture economics in India: capital cost, operating expenses, yield returns, and profitability analysis

This article presents a practical economic analysis of polyhouse floriculture in India, covering capital investment, operating costs, crop-wise yield potential, market pricing, and return on investment. The guide is designed for farmers, agripreneurs, and investors seeking realistic profitability estimates, payback periods, and risk considerations before establishing a commercial polyhouse floriculture unit.
