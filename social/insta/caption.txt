Lettuce cultivation under hydroponics: system selection, nutrient management, and yield optimization

This article provides a comprehensive, research-backed guide to growing lettuce under hydroponic systems. It covers suitable system designs (NFT, DWC, and substrate), environmental requirements, nutrient solution management, planting density, pest and disease control, and expected yields. Designed for Indian protected farming conditions, the guide emphasizes consistency, quality, and efficient water and nutrient use for commercial and small-scale growers.
