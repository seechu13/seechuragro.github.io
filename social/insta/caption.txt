Strawberry cultivation under polyhouse: varietal traits, climate management, and commercial fruit production

This article presents a research-grade protocol for cultivating strawberry (Fragaria × ananassa) under polyhouse conditions. It covers botanical characteristics, variety selection, polyhouse design, planting systems, climate and fertigation management, pest and disease control, harvesting practices, and yield potential. The guide is designed for commercial and semi-commercial growers targeting premium fresh fruit markets, improved fruit quality, and extended harvesting seasons under protected cultivation in India.
