Basil cultivation under NFT hydroponic system: botanical characteristics, climate requirements, and commercial production practices

Basil (Ocimum basilicum L.) is a premium culinary herb widely cultivated under NFT hydroponic systems due to its shallow root structure, rapid regrowth, and high market demand. This article presents a research-based guide covering botanical characteristics, climate and nutrient requirements, NFT system design, planting density, pruning practices, pest management, and yield expectations for professional basil production in controlled environments
