Polyhouse and Glass Greenhouse Cultivation: Choosing the Right Structure for Commercial Scale Farming

Polyhouses and glass greenhouses form the advanced tiers of protected cultivation, offering progressively higher levels of climate control, production stability, and commercial scalability. This article explores how polyethylene-covered polyhouses balance cost and control for fruiting vegetables, while glass greenhouses deliver near-complete environmental regulation for export-oriented and high-value crops. By examining structural design, climate systems, crop suitability, and economic logic, the article helps growers and investors select the most appropriate structure based on market goals, climate severity, and long-term operational capability.
