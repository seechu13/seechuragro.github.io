Milk Harvesting, Handling & Storage Systems: Quality Preservation from Udder to Chiller

Milk quality is determined not at the processing plant, but at the moment milk leaves the udder. Poor harvesting and handling practices silently degrade milk through microbial contamination, enzymatic activity, and temperature abuse. This chapter presents a PhD-grade, systems-based analysis of milk harvesting, handling, and storage—integrating udder physiology, hygiene science, equipment design, cooling technologies, and cold-chain discipline to preserve milk quality from farm to processor under Indian dairy conditions.
