Oyster Mushroom Cultivation in Polyhouse: Biology, Crop Cycle and Year-Round Production

Oyster mushroom cultivation under polyhouse conditions offers a reliable model for year-round production with minimal dependence on land quality or seasonal climate. This article explains the biological characteristics of oyster mushrooms, the cultivation process, substrate and spawn management, crop cycle behavior, yield patterns, and common pest and disease issues, providing a structured understanding of protected mushroom farming systems.
