Integrated Health & Veterinary Management Systems for Sustainable High-Performance Dairy Herds

Health is the central biological constraint that determines whether genetic potential, nutrition, and reproductive management translate into sustained dairy productivity. Modern dairy health management extends beyond disease treatment to integrated preventive systems encompassing biosecurity, vaccination, metabolic regulation, transition cow physiology, calf immunity, and data-driven veterinary oversight. This chapter provides a research-grade analysis of how structured health systems reduce production losses, stabilize fertility, extend productive lifespan, and convert veterinary science into long-term economic resilience for commercial dairy enterprises
