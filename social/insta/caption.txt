Indeterminate tomato cultivation in grow bags under polyhouse: botanical basis, climate control, and professional production practices

Indeterminate tomato hybrids form the backbone of commercial polyhouse cultivation due to their continuous growth habit and extended harvest potential. This article explains the botanical basis of indeterminate tomato growth and presents a professional guide to grow bag cultivation under polyhouse conditions. It covers climate and light requirements, grow bag media selection, fertigation and irrigation strategies, training and pruning systems, pest and disease management, yield expectations, and long-duration crop management principles for consistent productivity.
