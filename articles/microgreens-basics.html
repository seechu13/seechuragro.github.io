Microgreens 101: Fast Cycles, Big Flavor

Microgreens Published: Nov 6, 2025 Reading: — By Seechur Agro — Editorial Team

Microgreens are a rapid-return crop with sow-to-harvest cycles under two weeks for many species. They offer flavour intensity, colour, and nutrition — prized by chefs and health-focused consumers.

Why growers like them

Short cycle: Frequent harvests smooth cash flow.
Compact space: Vertical racks multiply output per m².
Predictable SKUs: Pea shoots, radish, sunflower, basil, etc.
Local freshness: Just-in-time delivery to HoReCa/retail.

Production notes

  • Sanitation: Clean seed, trays, and water to avoid damping-off.
  • Uniformity: Even seeding density and stack/unstack timing.
  • Climate: Temp & RH control; gentle airflow to keep foliage dry.
  • Harvest & pack: Sharp blades, food-safe handling, rapid chilling.

Commercial tips

  • Lock weekly standing orders with restaurants for base volume.
  • Offer sampler packs; rotate varieties seasonally.
  • Use clamshells with breathable liners; add harvest date & lot ID.
