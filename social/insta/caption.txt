Value-Added Dairy Products: Curd, Fermented Milks, and Market-Driven Processing Systems

Value addition transforms milk from a perishable commodity into a differentiated, higher-margin food product. Among dairy products, curd and fermented milks represent the most biologically efficient and culturally accepted pathways for value creation in India. This chapter presents a PhD-grade, systems-based analysis of fermented dairy products—covering microbiology, processing protocols, shelf-life control, scaling from cottage units to commercial plants, and the strategic potential of products such as laban and drinking yogurt in evolving dairy markets.
