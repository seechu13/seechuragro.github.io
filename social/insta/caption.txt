Indoor Saffron (Crocus sativus) Cultivation: A Research-grade Guide to Controlled-Environment Production

Indoor saffron cultivation (Crocus sativus) adapts an ancient, high-value crop to modern controlled-environment agriculture. By precisely managing temperature, photoperiod, moisture and root environment (soil, substrate, hydroponic or aeroponic), growers can produce consistent, high-quality stigmas year-after-year, expand saffron production into non-traditional geographies, and reduce climate-driven crop failures. This report synthesizes biological requirements, practical facility designs, planting systems, operating schedule, economics and mitigation for key risks.
