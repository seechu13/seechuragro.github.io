Feed & Nutrition Systems: Ration Design, Metabolic Efficiency, and Economics in Modern Dairy Herds

Feed and nutrition are the most powerful levers controlling milk yield, animal health, fertility, and farm profitability. Modern dairy nutrition extends far beyond feed quantity, integrating rumen microbiology, metabolic signaling, nutrient partitioning, and economic efficiency. This chapter presents a research-grade analysis of how rations should be designed, evaluated, and optimized across physiological stages to maximize milk production, protect animal health, and convert feed cost into predictable biological and financial returns.
