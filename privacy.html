Privacy Policy

Effective date: December 2, 2025

Introduction

Seechur Agro Private Limited ("Seechur", "we", "us") operates seechuragro.in. We design and implement protected cultivation and hydroponic projects and provide B2B agricultural services. This policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect personal and farm-related data in India.

Scope — who this covers

This policy applies to:

  • Visitors to our website;
  • Farmers and enterprise clients who request project quotes or services;
  • Suppliers and service providers who interact with us for operational purposes.

Data we collect

Depending on your interaction, we may collect:

  • Contact & identification: name, company name, email, phone.
  • Project & farm data: farm location (village/district), farm size, crop preferences, soil/irrigation details, photos, yield estimates provided by you for project planning.
  • Commercial data: quotations, orders, invoices and transaction metadata where applicable.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser, device, analytics and server logs.
  • Cookies: small tracking files used for analytics and session management.

How we use data

We use data to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide quotes, proposals and project delivery;
  • Arrange sampling, site visits and logistics for cultivation projects;
  • Operate and improve our website and business systems (analytics, security, communications);
  • Comply with accounting, tax and regulatory obligations in India.

Legal basis & Indian context

We process personal data where necessary to perform contracts with you, to comply with legal obligations, or on the basis of your consent. We follow industry best practices and will adopt applicable legal requirements as they become effective in India.

Sharing & third parties

We may share data with trusted service providers (hosting, analytics, accounting, logistics and supplier partners) under contract, with authorities where required by law, or with third parties where you provide consent. We do not sell personal data to marketing lists or data brokers.

Cross-border transfers

If we transfer data outside India (for example to a cloud host region), we will maintain contractual safeguards and only transfer what is necessary for the service.

Data retention

Typical retention timelines:

  • Enquiry & contact records: up to 3 years after last contact;
  • Project and commercial records: up to 7 years (to meet tax and accounting needs);
  • Analytics and logs: retained in aggregated/anonymised form as needed for site health and improvements.

Cookies & analytics

We use cookies and analytics (e.g., Google Analytics) to measure site traffic and improve user experience. You can disable cookies in your browser or use any opt-out provided by service providers.

Security

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the data. No system is 100% secure — please avoid sharing highly sensitive personal information via unencrypted email.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing or export of your data where legally permitted. To exercise your rights contact: privacy@seechuragro.in. We may request identity verification before processing such requests.

Children

Our services are intended for business users and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data of children. If you believe we have collected data about a minor, please contact us to request removal.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be signalled by revising the effective date above. Please check this page periodically for updates.

Contact
